## Safe current progression
**Lighthouse → Forest → Desert → Rocks → Volcano → Ending**

## Suggested checkpoints
These are route recommendations, NOT official fail conditions:
- Lighthouse: ~5–6 min
- Forest: ~13–15 min
- Desert: ~26–30 min
- Rocks: ~44–45 min
- Volcano + ending: target ~55 min; must finish below 60 for Bean

## Route material

### Lighthouse
Current speedrun-oriented guides recommend:
- Farm enough early money (one recent route uses ~60 Clams).
- Buy Crab Fishing Rod.
- Knife can be useful; an early upgrade is optional.
- Beer → Empty Beer Can → Spider Crab.
- Complete opening progression → Boat Keys/access + Radar.
⚠️ Money amounts and upgrade choices are route recommendations, not achievement requirements.

### Forest
✅ Collect 3 Leeches → NPC → special Leech bait → Giant Piranha → Skeleton hand-in → Desert.
⚠️ Inventory upgrade, Pistol, Dynamite etc. are route choices, not required by Bean.

### Desert
✅ Endangered catch → Tourist → Carrot → Pufferfish → Pufferfish Fin → Rocks.
Current speedrun guides may recommend SMG and weapon upgrades.
⚠️ Blue Shark/grill/money routing can vary by strategy. Do not present a specific farming plan as mandatory unless independently verified as required.

### Rocks
✅ Professional Boss Lure → Tuna.
✅ Place Tuna body to summon Albatross.
✅ Albatross Head hand-in → Volcano.
⚠️ Preserving Albatross meat/resources for Volcano is optimization advice, not a story requirement.

### Volcano
✅ Scientist quest → Fish Bucket → Bowhead Whale.
✅ Preserve Bowhead Whale body and take it to the volcano.
✅ Use it to summon Mutated Bowhead Whale.
✅ Defeat final boss → collect final quest drop/Whale Fin → return to Scientist.
✅ Receive RHIB/military-boat keys → depart for mainland → ending.
⚠️ Weapon/healing loadout is strategy advice.
