## Progression details

### Lighthouse → Forest
✅ Empty Beer Can summons Spider Crab.
✅ Defeat Spider Crab and complete the opening progression to gain boat access/keys and use the Radar for Forest.
⚠️ Sources disagree on the exact displayed name of the Spider Crab quest remains/drop. “Spider Crab Shell” is common in guides, but if the UI name is not independently confirmed, write **Spider Crab quest drop/remains**.
❌ Do not fabricate an exact item name just to make the prose sound certain.

### Forest → Desert
✅ Collect exactly **3 Leeches** from the ground.
✅ Give them to the woman by the Forest lake.
⚠️ The returned special bait is called **Leech Bait** in some game-record-style evidence and **Modified Leech** in many guides. Safe wording: **Leech Bait (often called Modified Leech in guides)**.
✅ Use it to summon Giant Piranha.
✅ Defeat Giant Piranha and return the **Giant Piranha Skeleton** to the NPC.
✅ This unlocks the Desert destination.

### Desert → Rocks
✅ The Tourist wants a Desert catch marked **Endangered / Endangered Species**.
✅ This is not uniquely Needlefish; Seahorse is also confirmed as a valid example.
✅ Standard Lure is used for the relevant Desert regular catch pool.
✅ Give a valid endangered catch to the Tourist → receive **Carrot**.
✅ Use Carrot to summon Pufferfish.
✅ Defeat Pufferfish → collect **Pufferfish Fin** → return it to the Tourist.
✅ This unlocks Rocks.
❌ Do not call the formal quest item “Pufferfish Tail”.

### Rocks → Volcano
✅ Use **Professional Boss Lure** to summon/catch Tuna.
✅ Do not sell/cook the Tuna; place the Tuna body on the ground/grass to summon Albatross.
✅ Defeat Albatross → collect **Albatross Head**.
✅ Give the head to the relevant Rocks NPC/shop quest NPC.
✅ This unlocks the Volcano destination.
