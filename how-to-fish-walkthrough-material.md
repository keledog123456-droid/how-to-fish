## Main progression
✅ **Lighthouse → Forest → Desert → Rocks → Volcano → Ending**

The game allows sandbox activity, but this is the core story progression.

## Lighthouse
Goal: unlock boat travel and reach Forest.

Core sequence:
1. Build early money/equipment as needed.
2. Obtain Beer / Empty Beer Can for the story encounter.
3. Use Empty Beer Can to summon Spider Crab.
4. Defeat Spider Crab.
5. Complete the opening quest/progression to obtain boat access/keys and Radar.
6. Follow the Forest Radar contact.

⚠️ Exact optimal purchases are recommendations, not mandatory story steps.
⚠️ Avoid asserting an unverified exact UI name for Spider Crab remains/drop.

## Forest
Goal: unlock Desert.

Core sequence:
1. Find the woman by the lake.
2. Collect exactly **3 Leeches from the ground**.
3. Give them to her.
4. Receive special Leech bait (safe terminology: **Leech Bait / often called Modified Leech**).
5. Use it to summon Giant Piranha.
6. Defeat Giant Piranha.
7. Return the **Giant Piranha Skeleton** to the quest NPC.
8. Follow the Desert destination.

Optional collection/miniboss content includes Sunfish and The Old Pike; do not confuse these with the required Giant Piranha progression chain.

## Desert
Goal: unlock Rocks.

Core sequence:
1. Speak with the Tourist.
2. Catch a Desert creature marked **Endangered / Endangered Species** using the relevant regular lure pool.
3. Valid examples include Seahorse; Needlefish is also reported as a valid example.
4. Give a valid endangered catch to the Tourist.
5. Receive **Carrot**.
6. Use Carrot to summon Pufferfish.
7. Defeat Pufferfish.
8. Collect **Pufferfish Fin**.
9. Return it to the Tourist.
10. Follow the Rocks destination.

Optional/miniboss content includes Blue Shark.
❌ Do not tell players Needlefish is the only acceptable endangered catch.

## Rocks
Goal: unlock Volcano.

Core sequence:
1. Acquire **Professional Boss Lure**.
2. Use it to summon/catch Tuna.
3. Defeat Tuna and preserve its body.
4. Place/drop the Tuna body on the ground/grass to summon Albatross.
5. Defeat Albatross.
6. Collect **Albatross Head**.
7. Give it to the relevant Rocks quest NPC/shop NPC.
8. Follow the Volcano Radar contact.

❌ Do not tell players to sell/cook the Tuna before summoning Albatross.

## Volcano
Goal: finish the story.

Current cross-checked sequence:
1. Reach Volcano after the Albatross Head hand-in.
2. Find the Scientist in the yellow hazmat suit.
3. Scientist requests **five fish**.
4. Hand over five fish; current guide evidence says any fish can satisfy this objective.
5. Receive **Fish Bucket**.
6. Use Fish Bucket to summon Bowhead Whale.
7. Defeat Bowhead Whale.
8. Preserve and carry the **entire Bowhead Whale body**.
9. Take it up the wooden path to the volcano crater.
10. Throw/use the Bowhead Whale in the volcano to summon **Mutated Bowhead Whale**.
11. Defeat Mutated Bowhead Whale.
12. Collect **Whale Fin**.
13. Return Whale Fin to the Scientist.
14. Receive keys to the **RHIB / military boat**.
15. Enter the RHIB and leave for the mainland.
16. This completes the main story and triggers the ending/credits.

✅ Current guide evidence says the same save remains playable after credits for unfinished collections/achievements.

## Equipment advice
Keep this visually separate from mandatory steps.
Possible recommendations:
- Upgrade ranged weapon damage/ammo before harder bosses.
- Carry healing food.
- Buy the lure tier available at each new progression stage.
- Preserve quest bodies/items when the next encounter requires them.
⚠️ Do not turn a recommended SMG/AR/loadout into a false hard requirement.
